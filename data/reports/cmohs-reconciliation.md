# CMOHS reconciliation

Comparison of this archive with the Congressional Medal of Honor Society recipient directory at <https://www.cmohs.org/recipients>. The directory was read from the newest Internet Archive captures of the listing pages (the index page from 28 August 2026, and pages 2–296 mostly from 4 February 2026). The live site is behind a bot check, so a full live crawl was not possible. The live “12 most recent presentations” page was checked separately and did not name anyone who is still absent after this update.

The archived directory has 3,547 cards and 3,546 unique recipient pages. This archive, after the data-correction merge on `main` (`b65dc05`) and before these additions, had **3,515 people** and **3,532 awards**. After these additions it has **3,540 people** and **3,557 awards**.

Names were matched on last name, first name, middle initial, action year, and conflict, with spelling differences such as Mc/Mac, De/De , and reversed index names treated as the same person. Those people were not added again. Examples already on file under the Army index spelling: Orren Bennett, Welis H. Blodgett, Frank G. Butterfield, Frank Burke, Josephus S. Cecil, Mike Colalillo, Banjamin C. Criswell, Joseph H. De Castro, Charles H. De Puy, James M. Elision, Nick Erickson, Joshija B. Hartzog, Anthony L. Krotiak, Angelo J. Liteky, Pitsenbarger William H., Ben L. Salomon, M. Sando Vargas Jr., Dr Mary E. Walker, Cavalry M. Young, Le Roy Williams, James B. Doshier, and Paul H. Welnert.

## Added

Twenty-five recipients. Posthumous flags were checked on the linked CMOHS page. Citation text is from the government source in the first URL. The CMOHS link is a reference only.
